# The Canadian Chef (TCC): image prompts

Paste the STYLE block at the end of every item prompt so all photos match your posters.
Generate each at 4:3 (menu cards) or 3:4 (posters). Keep the food centred with room around it.

## STYLE (append to every prompt)
Ultra realistic professional food photography, dark rustic wooden table, warm moody side lighting with soft rim light, shallow depth of field, rich saturated colours, visible steam and glistening texture, dark blurred restaurant background with warm bokeh, sharp focus on the food, 85mm lens, high detail, no text, no logos, no watermark.

## Combo posters (optional, same look as your existing ones)
Bold yellow-and-red brush-style headline, black background, food photo as the hero, round yellow price badge, phone number bar at the bottom. Text rendering in image generators is unreliable, so generate the food photo alone and add the headline, price and phone number (0787313830) in Canva or similar.

## Tips
- Generate 4 variations per item and pick the best.
- Add the TCC logo, prices and any text afterwards, not in the prompt.
- Avoid real brand names (Pepsi, Big Mac) in the prompt; the descriptions above get the same look without trademarks.
- Save files as `burger-tcc.jpg`, `shawarma-special.jpg` and so on. Then I can wire them into the app by filename.
